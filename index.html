Second Coat

A nail art site for people like me who can’t draw a straight line and have accepted that.

Purpose

I’m building this site because every nail tutorial I’ve tried to follow online assumes I already have steady hands and a full drawer of tools. I don’t. So this is the site I actually wish I’d had: a handful of designs that still look good even when your hand shakes a little, done with regular polish and whatever’s already in my drawer.

I’m keeping the scope small on purpose, mostly because I know myself and I’ll never finish this if I try to cover everything. No gel lamps, no acrylics, no reviewing every brand of polish out there. Just the basics done properly, plus a few designs that are forgiving of mistakes.

If a design needs a professional’s hand to look right, it’s not going on this site.
